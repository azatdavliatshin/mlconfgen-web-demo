<MLCONFGEN/>

Equivariant Diffusion · In Your Browser

Generate 3D molecular conformers from a shape constraint — no server, no upload. The model runs entirely client-side on your own machine via ONNX Runtime Web.

Load Model Weights

Why you supply the weights. The trained weights are licensed CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 and are not redistributed by this demo or published to npm. You download them once from Hugging Face and load them from your own disk. They are read directly into this browser tab — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

  1. Open the model repo

    Go to huggingface.co/Membrizard/ml_conformer_generator and accept the license terms to enable downloads.

  2. Download two ONNX files

    egnn_chembl_15_39.onnx (94 MB) and adj_mat_seer_chembl_15_39.onnx (83 MB). Grab both from the Files tab.

  3. Drop them below

    Drag each file onto its slot, or click to browse. They stay on your machine — loading is instant and works offline afterwards.
